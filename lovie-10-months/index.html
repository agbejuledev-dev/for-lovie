04 / JUST BETWEEN US

No fancy words, just me

My Lovie, listen…

Ten months. 🥹 I wish I could be there in person, steal a hug, look at your face while you talk, and do the little ordinary things that distance keeps turning into big plans.

I know saying “I love you” is beautiful, but I also know love needs effort behind it. I want to be more intentional, to listen properly, to remember the little things, and to make you feel loved in ways that go beyond a message on your phone.

You don't have to be okay every time we talk. You can tell me when you're tired, when you're worried, when you need reassurance, or when I could have done better. I want us to be able to be honest with each other and still feel safe choosing each other.

Thank you for these ten months, for the laughs, for the softness, for the lessons, and for being you. I don't take what we're building lightly.

Happy ten months, baby. I love you. ♡
