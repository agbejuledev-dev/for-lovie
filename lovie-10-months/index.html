04 / A LETTER TO YOU

Read this slowly, my love

My Lovie, my person.

My Lovie,

Happy ten months, baby. I wish I could be there beside you, look into your eyes, hold you close, and give you something you could touch and keep. Since I can't give you that physical moment right now, I wanted to make something that carries a little piece of my heart to you.

I need you to know that my love for you isn't only about what I can buy, what I can do on my best days, or how much I have figured out. It's in the intention. It's in remembering you, checking on you, learning you, praying for you, and choosing to keep trying even when life gets loud or distance makes things harder.

I'm proud of you, my girl. Proud of the person you are and the person you're becoming. I hope you give yourself credit for how far you've come. You deserve gentleness, good things, peace of mind, and a love that doesn't make you question whether you matter.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect. I can promise to keep being honest with you, to listen, to grow, and to do my part. If life ever gets difficult, I don't want us to forget that we're people learning together—not people who have to get everything right every time.

I love you in the loud, happy moments, and I want to love you well in the quiet, complicated ones too. Thank you for being my Lovie. Thank you for these ten months. And thank you for letting me be part of your world.

Always your Tobi. ♡
